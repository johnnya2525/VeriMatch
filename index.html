VeriMatch

Search works when you already know the card you want. VeriMatch helps when you don’t: it starts from a person, finds shared players and teams, then suggests a trade that works for both sides.

How it plugs in

  • Collection: uses the cards collectors have already added. Nothing new to enter.
  • Wish list: what each side wants drives the trade fit. Players and teams they follow are optional.
  • Trades: a match ends in a suggested offer that opens the existing offer flow with cards and cash prefilled. Verification and shipping stay exactly as they are.

Why it should lift engagement

  • A quick daily swipe gives collectors a reason to open the app between trades.
  • A match starts a conversation before an offer, so more traders turn into trade partners.
  • Cards that never get searched get seen by the people most likely to want them.
  • It pairs with Trade Night: see who shares your interests before the event.

What I would measure

Matches per session, match to offer rate, offer to completed trade rate, 7-day return rate, and trades per active collector. These are hypotheses to test, not results.
